Welcome home

Mountain modern, built for four seasons.

Set on a treed 0.39-acre lot in The Woodlands, this newly built residence pairs a clean, contemporary exterior with the warmth of walnut cabinetry, natural stone, and brushed brass. The main level lives on one floor — primary suite, office, great room, and kitchen all under 10-foot ceilings — while the upper level keeps guests, kids, and gear comfortably out of the way.

Every system was specified for McCall winters: a 150 psf snow-load roof structure, ribbed metal roofing, deep insulation, and a heated, oversized three-car tandem garage with room for sleds, skis, and a truck.
